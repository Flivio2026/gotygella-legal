Privacy Policy

GOTY Gella Clips is a private tool operated by a single person (the channel owner) to prepare and upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we process

  • TikTok authorization data (access token) of the channel owner, used only to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • Video files created by the owner. They are stored on the owner's own server.

What we do not do

We do not collect data from other users, do not sell or share data with third parties and do not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and files can be deleted at any time by the owner. Revoking the app in TikTok settings ends all access.
