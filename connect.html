Connect TikTok

GOTY Gella Clips is a private tool. It lets the channel owner send their own, manually approved short videos to their own TikTok inbox. Nothing is posted automatically.

The app asks for two permissions: user.info.basic (to identify the connected account) and video.upload (to send a video to the account's inbox as a draft). With ?stats=1 it also asks for read access to the account's own follower and video statistics (user.info.stats, video.list).

After you approve, you are sent to a page that shows a one-time code. Send that code to the private Telegram bot to finish.
